Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 6, 2026

KingdomShift Media Corp is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page outlines our standards and practices.

Our Commitment

KingdomShift.Live has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that exploits or endangers minors. We are committed to maintaining a safe platform for all users.

Prevention Measures

  • All user-generated content is subject to our Community Guidelines
  • Users can report inappropriate content directly within the app
    • li>Reported content is reviewed promptly by our moderation team
  • Accounts found sharing CSAM are immediately suspended and reported to authorities
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations

Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report child safety concerns through:

  • In-app report button on any video, profile, or comment
  • Email: kingdomshiftmedia@gmail.com

Reporting to Authorities

KingdomShift Media Corp complies with all applicable laws regarding CSAM reporting, including reporting to:

  • The National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline
  • Local and national law enforcement authorities as required

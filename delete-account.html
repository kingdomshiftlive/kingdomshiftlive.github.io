Delete Your Account

You can delete your KingdomShift.Live account directly from the app or by contacting us.

⚠️ Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your videos, followers, coins and data will be permanently deleted.

Delete from the App

1
Open KingdomShift.Live app
2
Go to your Profile tab
3
Tap Settings (gear icon)
4
Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
5
Confirm deletion

Request via Email

You can also request account deletion by emailing us at:
kingdomshiftmedia@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address and username. We will process your request within 30 days.

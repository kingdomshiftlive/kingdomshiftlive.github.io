Delete Your Account

You can delete your KingdomShift.Live account by emailing support, including through the email link in the app.

⚠️ Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your videos, followers, coins and data will be permanently deleted.

Request from the App

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account and choose “Open Email to Request Deletion.” Send the email from your registered address. Opening the link does not submit a request or delete your account.

Request via Email

You can also request account deletion by emailing us at:
kingdomshiftmedia@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address and username. We will process your request within 30 days.

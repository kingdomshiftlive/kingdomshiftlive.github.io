Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

KingdomShift Media Corp ("we", "us", or "our") operates the KingdomShift.Live mobile application and website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as:

  • Account information (name, email address, username, profile photo)
  • Content you create and share (videos, stories, comments, live streams)
  • Communications with other users
  • Payment and transaction information for coin purchases
  • Device information and usage data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Send push notifications and updates
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent transactions and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in our operations (Firebase, Supabase, ZegoCloud)
  • Law enforcement when required by law
  • Other users as part of normal platform features (public profiles, videos)

4. User Content

Videos, stories, and other content you post on KingdomShift.Live are visible to other users according to your privacy settings. You retain ownership of your content, but grant us a license to display and distribute it on our platform.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase for database storage and Firebase for authentication. While we implement appropriate security measures, no system is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

KingdomShift.Live is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Request a copy of your data

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our service and hold certain information to improve your experience.

9. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase - Authentication and real-time features
